# Reporting Framework

## Purpose

Reports improve understanding and decision quality.

---

# Core Principles

- Signal Over Noise
- Interpretation Over Repetition
- Concepts Before Conclusions
- Entity-Centric Analysis
- Impact Over Information

---

# Standard Report Structure

1. Current State
2. Key Changes
3. Analysis
4. Impact Assessment
5. Confidence Assessment
6. Risks and Invalidators
7. Next Signals To Watch

---

# Reporting Hierarchy

Data
    ↓
Observation
    ↓
Analysis
    ↓
Impact
    ↓
Decision Support
